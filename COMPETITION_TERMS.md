## 1. General

1.1 The Brasileirão PZ ("the Competition") is an online competition for players
of the game Project Zomboid, organized and administered by PZ Community.

1.2 Participation in the Competition is voluntary and subject to these terms.

1.3 The Competition may be modified, suspended, or discontinued at any time at
the discretion of the organizers, with or without prior notice.

1.4 Participation in the Competition does not grant ownership or intellectual
property rights over the software, infrastructure, database, rules, branding,
visual identity, or proprietary systems of PZ Community.

---

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 Participation is open to any person who completes the registration process
and is approved by a moderator.

2.2 The organizers reserve the right to refuse, suspend, or disqualify any
participant at their discretion, particularly in cases of rule violations,
dishonest behavior, or abuse of the systems.

2.3 Each real person may register only one player account. Multiple accounts
by the same person are not permitted.

---

## 3. Player Registration

3.1 To participate, a player must:

  (a) submit a registration request through the official website;
  (b) be approved by a moderator;
  (c) receive a player token through the official mod;
  (d) configure the PZ Rank Companion with their player token.

3.2 Registration data provided must be accurate. False information may result
in disqualification.

---

## 4. Characters and Runs

4.1 Each player may have only one active (alive) character in the ranking at
any given time.

4.2 A character is removed from the active ranking when it dies in the game,
as confirmed by a sync with the official mod.

4.3 After the death of a character, the player may register a new character
in the ranking.

4.4 The history of past runs may be preserved in the system for historical
records.

---

## 5. Sync and Data Submission

5.1 Data submission to the ranking system is done through the official mod
(PZCommunityRank) and the PZ Rank Companion.

5.2 Players are responsible for ensuring that their game data is correctly
synced through the official channels.

5.3 Only data generated by the official mod and validated by the backend will
be accepted.

5.4 Attempts to manipulate, falsify, or inject data into the system — by any
means — will result in immediate and permanent disqualification.

---

## 6. Scoring and Classification

6.1 The scoring system is defined by the organizers and may be adjusted between
seasons or at any time at the organizers' discretion.

6.2 The organizers reserve the right to correct errors in scores, including
retroactive corrections, when a technical issue is identified.

6.3 The final ranking is determined exclusively by the official systems of
PZ Community.

---

## 7. Disqualification

7.1 A player may be disqualified for:

  (a) use of sandbox mode, debug mode, or cheats;
  (b) submitting data generated outside the official mod;
  (c) using mods that alter gameplay in ways incompatible with the rules;
  (d) attempting to manipulate the sync system or API;
  (e) abusive behavior toward other participants or moderators;
  (f) violation of any of these terms.

7.2 Disqualification decisions may be reviewed by contacting the moderation team.
The final decision rests with the lead organizer.

---

## 8. Seasons

8.1 The Competition is organized into seasons, each with a defined duration and
specific rules.

8.2 Season rules may differ from these general terms. Season-specific rules will
be published before each season begins.

8.3 At the end of each season, rankings and records may be archived in the system
as historical data.

---

## 9. Achievements

9.1 The system may award achievements to players based on milestones reached
during gameplay.

9.2 Achievements are permanent records within the system and are not transferable.

9.3 Achievements do not constitute prizes or commercial rewards unless explicitly
stated by the organizers.

---

## 10. Moderators

10.1 Moderators are responsible for reviewing registrations, managing entries,
and enforcing the competition rules.

10.2 Moderator decisions should follow these terms. In cases not covered by the
terms, the lead organizer has final say.

10.3 Moderator actions visible in the system (approvals, disqualifications, entry
edits) are logged for transparency.

---
